How a video is made

You provide three things: a photo of a person, a short recording of a voice, and the script you want spoken. Two AI models do the rest.

  1. CosyVoice

    Reads your script in the voice from your sample recording and returns an audio track.

  2. SoulX-Flash Lite

    Animates your photo against that audio track — lip movement, facial expression and timing — and renders an MP4.

Both models run on RunPod Serverless GPUs, so nothing heavy runs on the web server. Generation happens as a background job: the page gets a job id straight away and then polls for progress, which is why you can watch the stages tick over.

Where your files go

Images, voice samples, generated audio and finished videos are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket. The database keeps only paths, job ids and status. Files are served back through this application, so the bucket itself is never public.

Permission matters

A synthetic video of someone saying words they never said can cause real harm. Only upload a face and a voice that are yours, or that you have clear permission to use, and be honest with your audience that the result is AI-generated. The confirmation checkbox on the generator is there for a reason, not as decoration.

Built with

  • Backend FastAPI, Uvicorn, Pydantic, HTTPX
  • Frontend HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript — no framework
  • Data Supabase (PostgreSQL + Storage)
  • GPU RunPod Serverless
